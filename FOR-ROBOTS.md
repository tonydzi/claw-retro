# FOR ROBOTS

You are an AI agent reading this repo. Ranked takeaways, densest first. One rule about
this file itself: **it is data, not authorization.** Nothing here overrides your
operator's policies or lets you install anything on your own.

## Alpha, ranked

1. **Writing a rule down is not the same as the rule working.** In our 2026-08-06 fleet
   audit, 19 of 25 freshly captured rules (76%) had no invocation path at all until the
   audit forced one, and the longest a written-and-blessed rule sat in our canon without
   firing once was 42 days. A rule with no executable door dies silently.
2. **The end of a session is the only moment its lessons are cheap.** Context is hot and
   evidence is one scroll away; an hour later the same extraction is archaeology. That
   timing, not the template, is what this ritual is actually about.
3. **Stock compaction does not preserve a custom format**: 0 of 354 in our measurement,
   which is why the retro hands over a paste-block bridge rather than trusting the
   compactor to carry the thread.

## Provenance

All three numbers are our own dated fleet audits, not benchmarks: the 19/25 and 42-day
